U.S. passports are issued by the **Department of State**, so the process is the same across the U.S. If this is your first passport, or you are applying for a child under 16, you must **apply in person** at an acceptance facility, usually a post office or a clerk of court's office. This article summarizes documents, fees and wait times.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **First-time applicants and children under 16:** apply in person with **Form DS-11**, and **don't sign it in advance**. Sign only when the agent tells you to.
> - **Adult fees (passport book):** **$130** application fee, paid to the Department of State, plus a **$35** execution fee paid to the acceptance facility. They are two separate payments.
> - **Children under 16:** generally **$100 + $35**, and the passport is valid for **5 years**.
> - **Wait times:** typically 4 to 6 weeks (routine) or 2 to 3 weeks (expedited, for an extra **$60**). These times **don't include mailing**.
> - **Children under 16:** **both parents** must appear, or one parent signs a notarized consent form, **DS-3053**.

## Who has to apply in person?

You apply in person with DS-11 if:

- This is your **first passport**.
- You are **under 16**.
- Your previous passport was lost, damaged, or isn't eligible for renewal by mail.

Someone with a previous passport who is eligible can **renew with Form DS-82**, which has the same application fee but **no $35 execution fee**.

## What to bring

- **Form DS-11**, filled in but **not signed**.
- **Proof of U.S. citizenship (originals):** a certified U.S. birth certificate, a **naturalization certificate**, a certificate of citizenship, or a Consular Report of Birth Abroad. If you naturalized, your [naturalization certificate](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/need-passport/apply-in-person.html) is your proof of citizenship. See [U.S. Birth Certificates](/en/blog/us-birth-certificate-certified-copy).
- **Photo ID** (such as a driver's license) and **a photocopy** of the front and back.
- **A passport photo** that meets the requirements.
- **Payment:** generally **two separate payments** (the application fee and the execution fee).

The full list is always on the [Department of State's page](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/need-passport/apply-in-person.html). Check it before you go.

## Fees (October 2026 fee chart)

| Type | Application fee | Execution fee |
|---|---|---|
| First-time adult passport book (DS-11) | $130 | $35 |
| Passport card | $30 | $35 |
| Book and card together | $160 | $35 |
| Child under 16, passport book | $100 | $35 |
| Expedited service (optional) | extra $60 | |

According to the [Department of State's fee chart](https://travel.state.gov/content/dam/tsg_aem/passports/documents/forms-fees/Passport_Fees_OCT_2026.pdf), the application fee goes to the Department of State and the execution fee goes to the acceptance facility. Fees can change, so check the latest chart before you apply.

## Wait times

The Department of State lists routine processing at **4 to 6 weeks** and expedited at **2 to 3 weeks**. Both figures **cover review only**. Mailing can take up to 2 weeks each way. If you need a passport urgently, the State Department's page says you can get an appointment at a passport agency when you have international travel within 14 days, with proof of the trip. **Don't buy airline tickets before you have the passport.**

## A passport for a child under 16

- **Both parents** (or guardians) must consent and **appear with the child** when applying.
- If one can't come, that parent signs **Form DS-3053** in front of a notary, with a copy of the ID shown to the notary.
- If **one parent has sole custody**, you submit proof such as a court order or a birth certificate listing only that parent.
- The child's birth certificate must list the parents' names to prove the relationship.
- A passport for a child under 16 is **valid for 5 years** and **can't be renewed by mail**.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a first U.S. passport cost?

For an adult passport book, generally a $130 application fee plus a $35 execution fee. For a child under 16, generally $100 plus $35. Expedited service adds $60. Check the Department of State's latest fee chart.

### Can I apply for my first passport by mail?

No. First-time passports and passports for children under 16 must be submitted in person at an acceptance facility with Form DS-11.

### How long does it take to get the passport?

Typically 4 to 6 weeks, or 2 to 3 weeks expedited, not counting mailing. Check the Department of State's page for current times.

### I just became a citizen. What proves my citizenship?

Your naturalization certificate is accepted as proof of citizenship when you apply for a passport.

### Who needs to be there when I apply for my child's passport?

Both parents appear with the child. If one can't come, that parent signs Form DS-3053 in front of a notary and you bring a copy of the ID they showed.

### How long is a child's passport valid?

A passport for a child under 16 is valid for 5 years and can't be renewed by mail.
