An EB-3 Other Workers case has four groups of costs. **PERM costs** are the prevailing wage, recruitment and attorney work on PERM. **Government fees** are Form I-140 and either the visa or Form I-485. The other two groups are medical exams and documents, and service fees charged by whoever helps with the case. The key rule: under **20 CFR 656.12(b)**, **the employer may not take money from the worker for anything related to getting PERM**, including the employer's attorney fees.

Figures checked October 19, 2026.

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - The employer pays PERM costs; taking money from the worker for PERM is prohibited.
> - Form I-140: $715 on paper or $665 online, plus the Asylum Program Fee.
> - Immigrant visa fee: $345 per person; Form I-485: $1,440 on paper or $1,390 online.

## PERM costs: the employer pays

According to [20 CFR 656.12 (Cornell LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/20/656.12), paragraph (b):

- The employer **may not seek or receive payment** for any activity related to obtaining labor certification (PERM), including the employer's attorney fees, whether as an incentive or as reimbursement.
- "Payment" is broad: cash, wage or benefit deductions, kickbacks, bribes, in-kind payments, and free labor.
- The worker may pay **their own** costs, such as their own attorney. But if **one attorney** represents both the employer and the worker, the employer must bear those costs.

If anyone asks you to "pay for PERM" or deducts recruitment costs from your wages, check with a licensed immigration attorney.

## Government fees

| Item | Fee | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form I-140 (the employer's petition) | $715 paper / $665 online | [USCIS G-1055 (edition 10/16/26)](https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055) |
| Asylum Program Fee with Form I-140 | $600; $300 for employers with 25 or fewer full-time-equivalent employees; $0 for nonprofits | USCIS G-1055 |
| Employment-based immigrant visa application (if interviewing abroad) | $345 per person | [U.S. Department of State](https://adoption.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/fees/fees-visa-services.html) |
| Form I-485 (if adjusting status in the U.S.) | $1,440 paper / $1,390 online, per person | USCIS G-1055 |

The $345 visa fee is **per person**, so a family of four interviewing in Ho Chi Minh City pays it four times. Fees can change; check before you file.

## Other costs

- **Medical exam** with a panel physician (abroad) or civil surgeon (in the U.S.). The clinic sets the price.
- **Civil documents**, police certificates, translations and notarizations.
- **Airfare** to the U.S. after the visa is issued.

## Service fees and attorney fees

Beyond government fees, whoever helps with the case usually charges for their work: coordination, translation, case tracking, and immigration attorney fees for the worker's legal work **outside PERM**. When you get a quote, ask for:

1. **An itemized list**: what goes to the government, what goes to the attorney, what is a service fee.
2. **A written contract** naming the legal entity and its address.
3. **Payment to a company account**, not to an individual, with receipts.
4. **Payments tied to case milestones**, not everything up front.
5. **A refund clause** in case the case cannot go forward.

Government fees go straight to USCIS or the State Department through official channels. USCIS never accepts Western Union, MoneyGram, PayPal or gift cards. See [EB-3 Scam Warning Signs](/en/blog/eb3-scam-warning-signs).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the worker pay PERM costs?

No. Under 20 CFR 656.12(b), the employer may not receive payment from the worker for PERM, including the employer's attorney fees, in any form.

### How much is the Form I-140 fee?

Under the USCIS G-1055 fee schedule (edition October 16, 2026): $715 on paper or $665 online, plus a $600 Asylum Program Fee ($300 for small employers, $0 for nonprofits).

### What is the EB-3 visa fee when interviewing in Vietnam?

The employment-based immigrant visa application fee is $345 per person, according to the State Department, not counting the medical exam.

### What are the fees to adjust status in the U.S.?

Form I-485 is $1,440 on paper or $1,390 online per person, under USCIS G-1055 (edition October 16, 2026).

### What should I check before paying someone to help with my case?

Ask for an itemized list, a written contract, payment to a company account with receipts, and payments tied to case milestones.

### Is it legal to deduct recruitment costs from my wages?

Under 20 CFR 656.12(b), wage or benefit deductions count as payment for PERM and are prohibited. Ask a licensed immigration attorney if this happens to you.
