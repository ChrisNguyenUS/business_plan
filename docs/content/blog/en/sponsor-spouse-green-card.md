Sponsoring your husband or wife for a green card starts with **Form I-130 (Petition for Alien Relative)** filed with USCIS. How long it takes depends on who is sponsoring:

- A **U.S. citizen's** spouse is an **immediate relative**. USCIS says: "Visas are always available for immediate relatives of U.S. citizens", so there is **no wait for a visa number**.
- A **green card holder's** spouse is in the **F2A** preference category and waits according to the **Visa Bulletin**. In the October 2026 Visa Bulletin, the F2A Final Action Date is **September 22, 2026**, and the Dates for Filing chart shows F2A as "C" (current).

The Form I-130 fee is $625 online or $675 on paper (G-1055 Fee Schedule, edition October 16, 2026). If your spouse applies for an immigrant visa abroad (in Vietnam, for example), there is also a $325 State Department visa application fee and a $120 Affidavit of Support review fee.

## Who can sponsor a spouse?

- **U.S. citizens** can sponsor a spouse. According to [Family of U.S. Citizens](https://www.uscis.gov/family/family-of-us-citizens), spouses of U.S. citizens are immediate relatives.
- **Permanent residents** can sponsor a spouse and unmarried children, according to [Family of Green Card Holders](https://www.uscis.gov/family/family-of-green-card-holders-permanent-residents). They wait based on the priority date, the date USCIS received a properly filed Form I-130.
- The marriage must be **legal** and **genuine**, not entered only to get a green card. You will need evidence of the relationship.

If you are a permanent resident who will soon qualify for naturalization, **becoming a citizen** moves your spouse's case into the immediate relative category. See [U.S. Citizenship Test 2026](/en/blog/us-citizenship-test-guide) and practice free with [N400 Ready](/en/n400ready).

## What are the steps?

### If your spouse is abroad (consular processing)

1. **File Form I-130** with USCIS, with proof of your citizenship or green card, your marriage certificate, proof that any prior marriages ended, and evidence that the marriage is genuine.
2. **USCIS approves Form I-130** and sends the case to the State Department's **National Visa Center (NVC)**.
3. **Pay NVC fees**: the **$325** immigrant visa application fee and the **$120** Affidavit of Support review fee, per the [State Department fee page](https://adoption.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/fees/fees-visa-services.html).
4. **Submit Form DS-260 and civil documents**, with **Form I-864 (Affidavit of Support)**. You, the sponsor, promise financial support; USCIS charges no fee for Form I-864.
5. **Medical exam and interview** at the U.S. embassy or consulate that handles immigrant visas for your spouse's country. For Vietnam, that is the U.S. Consulate General in Ho Chi Minh City.
6. **Enter the U.S.**, and the green card arrives by mail.

### If your spouse is lawfully in the U.S. (adjustment of status)

If your spouse is in the U.S. and eligible, they may file **Form I-485** to adjust status. Spouses of U.S. citizens often file Forms I-130 and I-485 together. The Form I-485 fee is $1,390 online or $1,440 on paper under the G-1055 Fee Schedule. Adjustment eligibility has many exceptions (how the person entered, time overstayed, and so on), so this path should be discussed with a licensed immigration attorney.

## How long does it take?

The time has two parts:

- **USCIS processing of Form I-130**: see [USCIS Processing Times](https://egov.uscis.gov/processing-times/).
- **The wait for a visa number** (F2A only), shown in the [State Department Visa Bulletin](https://adoption.state.gov/content/travel/en/legal/visa-law0/visa-bulletin/2027/visa-bulletin-for-october-2026.html), plus NVC processing and the consulate's interview schedule.

Under the October 2026 Visa Bulletin (checked October 19, 2026), Vietnam has no separate column, so the "All Chargeability Areas" column applies:

| Category | Who sponsors | Final Action Date (Oct 2026) | Dates for Filing (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate relative | U.S. citizen | Not applicable (visas always available) | Not applicable |
| F2A | Permanent resident | September 22, 2026 | Current (C) |

The Visa Bulletin changes every month. Check the latest one before estimating your timeline.

## 2-year or 10-year green card?

If the marriage is **less than 2 years old** on the day your spouse enters the U.S. or is approved for a green card, they receive a **2-year conditional green card**. Near the end of the 2 years, you file **Form I-751** together for a 10-year card. See [2-Year to 10-Year Green Card](/en/blog/remove-conditions-green-card-i-751).

## Documents to gather

- Proof of U.S. citizenship (passport, Certificate of Naturalization) or the sponsor's green card.
- Marriage certificate; divorce decrees or death certificates for any prior marriages.
- Evidence of a genuine marriage: photos over time, messages, travel records of visits, shared finances (if any).
- Financial documents for Form I-864: tax returns and proof of employment. If your income is not enough, you may need a joint sponsor.
- The beneficiary's civil documents: birth certificate, police certificate, passport.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to sponsor a spouse?

It includes USCIS processing of Form I-130, NVC processing and the consulate's interview schedule. Spouses of U.S. citizens don't wait for a visa number; spouses of permanent residents (F2A) wait according to the Visa Bulletin.

### What are the government fees to sponsor a spouse?

Form I-130: $625 online or $675 on paper (G-1055, edition October 16, 2026). For a visa abroad, add the $325 visa application fee and the $120 Affidavit of Support review fee (State Department), plus medical exam and document costs.

### Can a green card holder sponsor a spouse?

Yes, in the F2A category. In the October 2026 Visa Bulletin, the F2A Final Action Date is September 22, 2026.

### Where is the spouse visa interview for Vietnamese applicants?

Vietnamese immigrant visa applicants usually interview at the U.S. Consulate General in Ho Chi Minh City.

### Will my spouse get a 10-year green card right away?

If the marriage was less than 2 years old when the green card was granted, your spouse gets a 2-year conditional card and must file Form I-751 for a 10-year card.

### How much income do I need to sponsor a spouse?

You file Form I-864 and must meet the minimum income in USCIS guidelines (generally 125% of the federal poverty guidelines for your household size). If you fall short, you can use assets or a joint sponsor.

### What has changed recently in spouse sponsorship?

USCIS fees follow the G-1055 Fee Schedule (edition October 16, 2026), and F2A wait times change with each monthly Visa Bulletin. Check uscis.gov and the latest Visa Bulletin before you file.
