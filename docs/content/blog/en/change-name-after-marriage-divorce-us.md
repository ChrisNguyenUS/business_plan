After a marriage or divorce, many people want to change their name on their documents. In the U.S., a marriage certificate or divorce decree **doesn't automatically change your name** everywhere. You update **each place separately**. The usual starting point is your **Social Security card**, because many other agencies check names against SSA records. This article covers the general process across the U.S. Each state may have its own rules for driver's licenses.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Changing your name on your SSN card is free.** Your SSN **stays the same**; only the name changes.
> - You need **proof of the legal name change**: a marriage certificate, divorce decree, naturalization certificate showing the new name, or a court order. You also need **ID** such as a driver's license, state ID or U.S. passport.
> - Some states allow the change **online** if you married there. You must wait at least **30 days** after the wedding.
> - The new card usually arrives within **about 14 business days** after you finish.
> - After SSA, update your **driver's license (REAL ID), passport, bank and employer**.

## The order to follow

1. **SSA** (Social Security card).
2. **Driver's license or state ID** at the DMV.
3. **Passport** (if you have one).
4. **Employer, bank, insurance, credit cards.**

Doing SSA first usually helps because your name will then match federal records. Ask your state's DMV about the order it requires.

## Changing your name on the Social Security card

### Documents you need

SSA says you need:

- **Proof of the legal name change:** a marriage certificate, divorce decree, naturalization certificate showing the new name, or a court order. It must be an **original or a copy certified by the issuing agency**. SSA doesn't accept photocopies or notarized copies.
- **Current ID:** a driver's license, state-issued ID card or U.S. passport. If your name changed more than 2 years ago, SSA may also ask for ID in your **prior name** (an expired one may work).

### Online or at an office

SSA has an **online** option for certain marriage name changes. You need a current driver's license or state ID, and a marriage certificate issued by a participating state. You must **wait at least 30 days** after the wedding to apply. If you don't qualify, you start online and finish at an SSA office, or use **Form SS-5**. The list of participating states can change, so check [SSA's replace card page](https://www.ssa.gov/number-card/replace-card).

A name change **doesn't count toward the limit** on replacement cards (3 per year, 10 in a lifetime). For details, see [Getting a Social Security Number and Replacing a Lost Card](/en/blog/get-social-security-number-replace-card).

## Updating your driver's license and REAL ID

After SSA, go to your state's DMV to change the name on your license. Each state has its own document list. If you want a **REAL ID**, bring proof of residence and lawful status too. If the name on your birth certificate differs from your current name, bring the marriage certificate to link them. See [REAL ID and Driver's Licenses in the U.S.](/en/blog/real-id-driver-license-new-to-us).

## Passport and everywhere else

For a passport, check the Department of State's latest instructions for name changes. For your bank, employer, insurance and credit cards, tell them your new name after your SSN card and license are updated. **The name on your payroll and tax records should match SSA's records**, or you can run into problems when you file taxes. See [Your First U.S. Tax Return](/en/blog/first-us-tax-return-new-immigrants).

## What about changing your name in Vietnam?

Name changes in Vietnam, such as recording a change in civil status, are governed by Vietnamese authorities. This article covers U.S. documents only.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it cost anything to change my name on my Social Security card?

No. SSA doesn't charge for a name change on the card. Your SSN stays the same.

### What documents do I need to change my name after marriage?

An original or certified copy of your marriage certificate, and current ID such as a driver's license, state ID or U.S. passport.

### Can I change it online?

Some marriages in participating states can be handled online, and you must wait at least 30 days after the wedding. Otherwise you finish at an SSA office.

### How long until the new card arrives?

SSA says usually within about 14 business days after you complete the application.

### What do I update after SSA?

Your driver's license or state ID, your passport, and your employer, bank and insurance. The name on your payroll and tax records should match SSA's records.

### I divorced and want my previous name back. What do I do?

You use the divorce decree or a court order that includes the name change as your proof, along with ID, much like a name change after marriage.
