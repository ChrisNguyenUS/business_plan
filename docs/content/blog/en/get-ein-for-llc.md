An **EIN (Employer Identification Number)** is the 9-digit tax number the IRS gives a business. An LLC needs one to open a bank account, hire employees and file taxes. **The IRS issues EINs for free.** If the responsible party has an SSN or ITIN and the business is in the U.S., you apply online at IRS.gov and **get the number immediately**. If the business or its owner is abroad, you apply **by phone at 267-941-1099** or **by faxing Form SS-4**.

Figures are from the [IRS EIN page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) (updated October 16, 2026) and the [Form SS-4 instructions (12/2025)](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4), checked October 19, 2026.

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - The EIN is free from the IRS; never pay a website for the number itself.
> - With an SSN or ITIN and a U.S. business, apply online and get it immediately.
> - From abroad, call 267-941-1099 or fax Form SS-4; no ITIN is needed.

## Before you apply

- **Form the LLC with the state first.** The IRS says applying before the state accepts the company may delay your EIN. In Texas, see [How to Form an LLC in Texas](/en/blog/how-to-form-llc-texas).
- **Identify the "responsible party":** it must be an **individual**, usually the owner or the person who runs the LLC, not another company.
- **Know how many members the LLC has.** Form SS-4 asks, and the IRS treats single-member and multi-member LLCs differently for tax.

## Option 1: Apply online (SSN or ITIN, U.S. business)

IRS requirements:

- The business is formed in the U.S. (or a U.S. territory) and has its principal place of business there.
- You are the responsible party or their authorized representative, and you have the responsible party's **SSN or ITIN**.

Online tool hours (Eastern time):

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 6:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m. the next day |
| Saturday | 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. |
| Sunday | 6:00 p.m. to midnight |

Online tips:

- Finish in one session; you can't save and come back. **After 15 minutes of inactivity** the session expires and you start over.
- Each responsible party can get **one EIN per day**.
- If approved, the number appears at once. **Print the EIN confirmation letter**; banks usually ask for it.

## Option 2: No SSN or ITIN, or based abroad

According to the Form SS-4 instructions:

- **Phone:** international applicants call **267-941-1099** (not toll-free), Monday to Friday, 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastern. The IRS issues EINs by phone only to international applicants.
- **Fax:** send Form SS-4 to **304-707-9471** from outside the U.S., or **855-215-1627** from inside the U.S.
- **Mail:** send Form SS-4 to the address in the instructions. This is the slowest option.
- **No ITIN required:** foreign individuals don't need an ITIN to receive an EIN. On line 7b, enter "Foreign" or N/A if the responsible party doesn't have and isn't eligible for an SSN or ITIN.

Living in Vietnam? See [Forming a U.S. LLC from Vietnam](/en/blog/form-us-llc-from-vietnam).

## Don't pay a website to "get your EIN"

The IRS warns about websites that charge to get an EIN and says **you never have to pay a fee for an EIN**. The official site is IRS.gov. If you already paid a fake site, the IRS suggests disputing the charge with your bank or card company and reporting it to the FTC.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does an EIN cost anything?

No. The IRS issues EINs for free and warns about websites that charge for them.

### How long does it take to get an EIN online?

If you qualify to apply online and are approved, you get the EIN as soon as you finish, in one session.

### Can I get an EIN without an SSN?

Yes. International applicants call 267-941-1099 or fax Form SS-4. According to the IRS, foreign individuals don't need an ITIN to receive an EIN.

### How many EINs can I get in a day?

Each responsible party can get one EIN per day.

### Should I get the EIN before or after forming the LLC?

After. The IRS advises forming the company with the state first, or the EIN application may be delayed.

### Does a single-member LLC need an EIN?

An LLC needs an EIN if it has employees or must file certain taxes of its own, and most banks require one for a business account. Ask your tax preparer if you're unsure.

### What happens if my online session times out?

You start over. Online sessions expire after 15 minutes of inactivity and can't be saved.
