In the **October 2026 Visa Bulletin**, **EB-3 Other Workers** for people born in Vietnam (and most other countries) has a **final action date of January 1, 2022** and a **date for filing of June 1, 2022**. That means this month only cases with a **priority date before January 1, 2022** can get a visa or a green card approval. The priority date is usually the date the employer filed PERM. Before that date even exists, the case goes through the prevailing wage and recruitment steps, and PERM alone currently averages **336 days**.

The Visa Bulletin changes every month. Figures below were checked October 19, 2026.

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - October 2026 final action date for Other Workers (Vietnam and most countries): January 1, 2022.
> - PERM currently averages 336 days before Form I-140 can be filed.
> - No one can predict the Visa Bulletin or lawfully skip the line.

## Reading the Visa Bulletin if you were born in Vietnam

The **priority date** is the date the Department of Labor received the employer's PERM application. Vietnam has no column of its own, so people born there use **"All Chargeability Areas Except Those Listed"**. From the [October 2026 Visa Bulletin](https://adoption.state.gov/content/travel/en/legal/visa-law0/visa-bulletin/2027/visa-bulletin-for-october-2026.html):

| Category | Final Action Date | Date for Filing |
|---|---|---|
| EB-3 Skilled Workers and Professionals | May 15, 2024 | August 1, 2024 |
| **EB-3 Other Workers** | **January 1, 2022** | **June 1, 2022** |

- **Final Action Date:** cases with a priority date **before** this date can be issued a visa (at a consulate) or have Form I-485 approved (in the U.S.).
- **Date for Filing:** cases with a priority date before this date can start submitting documents to the National Visa Center. For applicants in the U.S., USCIS says each month which chart to use for filing Form I-485.

Other Workers waits longer than Skilled Workers because it gets at most 10,000 numbers a year, per the Visa Bulletin.

## What makes up the total time?

| Stage | Who handles it | Current figures |
|---|---|---|
| Prevailing wage | Department of Labor | Working on requests received June 2026 (as of September 30, 2026); no average published |
| Recruitment under the rules | Employer | Follows PERM recruitment steps |
| PERM | Department of Labor | Reviewing cases filed December 2025; average **336 days** (August 2026) |
| Form I-140 | USCIS | See [USCIS Processing Times](https://egov.uscis.gov/processing-times/) |
| Waiting for a visa number | Department of State | Depends on the monthly Visa Bulletin |
| Consular interview or I-485 | Consulate / USCIS | Depends on the Ho Chi Minh City consulate's or the USCIS office's schedule |

PERM and prevailing wage figures are from the [Department of Labor's processing times page](https://flag.dol.gov/processingtimes).

## An example of how the math works (not a prediction for your case)

Say the employer files PERM on March 1, 2027; the priority date is March 1, 2027. The visa can be issued only once the Visa Bulletin moves the Other Workers final action date past March 1, 2027. In October 2026 that date sat at January 1, 2022. No one knows in advance whether the bulletin will move fast, slowly, stand still or move back.

So estimates like "4 to 6 years" are based on past movement. The real wait can be longer.

## How to keep track

- Read each new Visa Bulletin on the State Department site: the "All Chargeability" column, the "Other Workers" row.
- If you're in the U.S., check USCIS's "Adjustment of Status Filing Charts" page to see which chart applies that month.
- Keep the documents that show your priority date (the PERM approval, the I-140 receipt).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the current EB-3 Other Workers date?

In the October 2026 Visa Bulletin, EB-3 Other Workers for Vietnam and most countries has a final action date of January 1, 2022 and a date for filing of June 1, 2022.

### Which Visa Bulletin column applies to people born in Vietnam?

"All Chargeability Areas Except Those Listed", because Vietnam has no separate column.

### What is a priority date?

For EB-3 cases that need PERM, it's usually the date the Department of Labor received the PERM application. The case is current when that date is before the final action date in the Visa Bulletin.

### How long does PERM take?

According to the Department of Labor, as of October 5, 2026 it was reviewing PERM cases filed in December 2025, averaging 336 days.

### Why does Other Workers wait longer than Skilled Workers?

Other Workers gets at most 10,000 numbers a year (minus a NACARA reduction), far fewer than demand.

### Is there a way to go faster?

There is no lawful way to skip the Visa Bulletin line. Anyone promising a faster route through "connections" is a red flag.
