# N-400 Interview Vocabulary: Quick English Definitions ("What Mean")

This guide provides the shortest, absolute must-know English definitions required for answering the "What does this mean?" questions during the USCIS naturalization interview.
1. Claim to be a U.S. citizen | Can you explain what claim to be a U.S. citizen means? : To say you are a U.S. citizen.
2. Register to vote | Do you understand what register to vote means? : Sign up to choose a leader.
3. Vote | Can you tell me what vote means? : To choose a leader in an election.
4. Owe overdue taxes | What is the meaning of owe overdue taxes? : Didn't pay taxes on time.
5. Nonresident | Can you define nonresident? : Someone who does not live in the U.S.
6. Alien | What does alien mean? : Someone who is not a U.S. citizen.
7. Overthrow | Can you explain what overthrow means? : To remove a government by force.
8. Unconstitutional means | What is the meaning of unconstitutional means? : Methods that go against the Constitution.
9. Weapon | Can you explain what a weapon is? : A knife or a gun.
10. Torture | What is the meaning of torture? : To cause great pain to someone.
11. Genocide | Do you know what genocide means? : To kill a group of people because of their religion or race.
12. Kill | What does it mean to kill? : To cause someone to die.
13. Prison / Jail | How would you explain prison or jail? : A place where the police keep people who have been arrested.
14. Detention facility | What does detention facility mean? : A place where people are forced to stay.
15. Labor camp | Can you define labor camp? : A place where people are forced to work.
16. Crime | Do you understand what a crime is? : An action that breaks the law.
17. Offense | Can you explain what offense means? : Minor crime.
18. Arrested | Do you know what arrested is? : Caught by the police.
19. Cited | What is the meaning of cited? : Given a ticket by the police.
20. Confined | What does confined mean? : Jailed by the police.
21. Prostitution | Can you define prostitution? : Having sex for money.
22. Smuggle | What is the meaning of smuggle? : To bring something illegally into the country.
23. Narcotics | How would you explain narcotics? : Illegal drugs such as heroin or cocaine.
24. Drug paraphernalia | Can you explain what drug paraphernalia means? : Equipment to make or use illegal drugs.
25. Obtain immigration benefit | Do you know what obtain immigration benefit is? : To gain things like a green card.
26. Illegal gambling | What does illegal gambling mean? : To play a game for money that is against the law.
27. Support your dependent | Can you explain what support your dependent means? : To pay child support.
28. Pay alimony | What does it mean to pay alimony? : To give money to an ex-spouse after a divorce.
29. Misrepresentation | Can you define misrepresentation? : Lying about who you are or something you did.
30. Public benefit | What is the meaning of public benefit? : Money from the government such as food stamps.
31. Fraudulent | Can you explain what fraudulent means? : To claim something that is not true.
32. Lie | Do you understand what lie means? : To say things that are not true.
33. Deported | What does it mean to be deported? : Forced to leave the U.S.
34. Constitution | Can you explain what the Constitution is? : The supreme law of the land.
35. Form of government of the U.S. | What is the form of government of the United States? : Republic.
36. Oath of allegiance to the United States | How would you explain the oath of allegiance? : A promise to be loyal to the United States.
37. Bear arms on behalf of the United States | Can you define bear arms on behalf of the United States? : To use a gun to defend the United States during a war.
38. Perform non-combatant services | Can you explain what perform non-combatant services means? : Do work that is not fighting in a war.
39. Example of non-combatant services | Give me an example of non-combatant services. : Nursing, cooking, or translation.
40. Civilian | What does civilian mean? : Anyone who is not in the military.
41. Perform work of national importance under civilian direction | What does perform work of national importance under civilian direction mean? : Do non-military work during a national crisis.
42. Example of work of national importance | Give me an example of work of national importance. : Helping the Red Cross after an earthquake.
43. Habitual drunkard | Can you explain what a habitual drunkard is? : A person who regularly drinks too much alcohol.
44. Polygamy | What does polygamy mean? : Married to more than one person at the same time.
45. Marital status | What is the meaning of marital status? : Being single, married, divorced, or widowed.
46. Spouse | Can you tell me what spouse means? : Husband or wife.
47. Annulled | What does annulled mean? : Marriage officially ended as if it never happened.
48. Perjury / Penalty of perjury | Do you know what penalty of perjury means? : To lie under oath.
49. Swear / Certify | What does it mean to swear or certify? : To promise to tell the truth.
50. Verify | Can you explain what verify means? : To prove something is true.
51. Pending | What is the meaning of pending? : Has not been decided yet.
52. Exempt | What does it mean to be exempt? : To not have to do something.
53. Militia | Can you define militia? : An army that does not work for an official government.
54. Insurgent organization | What is an insurgent organization? : A group that uses weapons to fight a government.
55. Vigilante unit | Can you explain what a vigilante unit is? : A group that acts like police but is not official police.
56. Alternative sentencing | What does alternative sentencing mean? : A punishment decided by law instead of jail time.
57. Rehabilitative program | Can you define rehabilitative program? : A program to help someone recover from drugs or alcohol.
58. Good Moral Character | How would you explain Good Moral Character? : Being a good person and following the law.
59. Naturalization | What does naturalization mean? : The process to become a U.S. citizen.
60. Prior / Previous | What is the meaning of prior? : Before.
61. Current | What does current mean? : Right now / Where you live now.
62. Disclose | Can you explain what disclose means? : To tell / To provide information.
